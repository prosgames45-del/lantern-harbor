THE KEEPER'S CONTRACT

A harbor worth returning to.

Finish the twelfth tide with 10 deliveries and 90 harbor coins. Trade for supplies, leave a reserve for upkeep and plan around storms.

How to keep the port alive
  • Skiffs need a quay and 1 fuel; they pay 8 coins.
  • Barges need a freight dock and 2 fuel; they pay 14 coins.
  • A vessel waits three tides before leaving. Each departure costs one reputation.
  • Storms damage unprotected harbors. A breakwater uses a bay but blocks every storm.
  • Every calm or tailwind tide delivers 2 fuel, up to a store of 10. Storms stop fuel deliveries even with a breakwater. Tailwinds add 3 coins per unloading.
  • Each tide costs 2 coins in harbor upkeep. A breakwater requires extra timber; protect the port or keep the money for repairs and fuel.
  • You can trade and repair before ringing the bell. A harbor with zero integrity or reputation closes.
