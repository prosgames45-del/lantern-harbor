## Description

Keep a small coastal harbor alive through twelve tides. Every bay is a choice: earn from fishing skiffs, invest in freight capacity, or give up dock space for a storm-proof breakwater. Supply fuel, pay upkeep, trade timber, and keep waiting vessels from leaving. Three repeatable scenarios vary storms and delivery/coin targets; a preview shows the consequences of advancing the tide. All in-game coins are fictional.

## Controls

Choose a structure from the list and tap an empty bay to build. Tap a waiting vessel to unload at a matching dock. Trade or repair before ringing the bell to advance the tide. Mouse, touch, Tab and Enter/Space are supported. New harbor restarts the same deterministic scenario.
